## Content Production Workflow

1. **Monday:** Outline 2 articles for the week (keywords, structure)
2. **Tuesday-Wednesday:** Write articles (1000-1500 words each)
3. **Thursday:** Review + SEO optimize (title, meta, headers, images)
4. **Friday:** Publish + share on social channels
5. **Daily:** 1 social post (queued via Buffer/Later)

## Uzbek Language Strategy

- All blog articles published in RU first
- Top-performing articles translated to UZ (based on traffic data after Month 1)
- Social posts: alternate RU and UZ (Mon/Wed/Fri = RU, Tue/Thu = UZ)
